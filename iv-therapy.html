The Clinical Standard

IV Therapy Without
the Trend

Intravenous therapy delivers nutrients, hydration, and therapeutic compounds directly into the bloodstream — bypassing gastrointestinal absorption entirely. This route of delivery can achieve concentrations and bioavailability that oral supplementation cannot.

At Epic Revive, IV protocols are not assembled from a menu. Each protocol is selected based on your current health status, biomarker data, and specific clinical objective. There are no novelty infusions here.

All IV therapy at Epic Revive is physician-overseen. Compounded preparations are sourced from licensed, FDA-registered compounding pharmacies. Every infusion follows a structured clinical protocol.

"Intravenous therapy is a medical procedure. At Epic Revive, it is treated as one." Ross Pavlik, M.D.
Clinical IV Protocols

Structured Infusion Programs

Each protocol below is designed around a specific clinical objective. Protocol selection and dosing are individualized following a physician consultation.

Foundation

Hydration & Electrolyte Restoration

Isotonic crystalloid infusion with individualized electrolyte correction. Appropriate for dehydration, post-travel recovery, gastrointestinal illness, and baseline optimization.

Base Components Lactated Ringer's or Normal Saline · Electrolyte panel · Magnesium
Micronutrient

High-Dose Vitamin C Protocol

Intravenous ascorbic acid at therapeutic concentrations for immune modulation, oxidative stress reduction, and collagen synthesis support. Clinical concentrations not achievable through oral supplementation.

Core Components Ascorbic acid (high-dose) · B-complex · Trace minerals
Metabolic

Myers' Cocktail & Micronutrient Infusion

A physician-modified formulation of the classic Myers' protocol including magnesium, B vitamins, vitamin C, and calcium. Applicable for fatigue, immune support, and baseline micronutrient replenishment.

Core Components Magnesium · B5 · B6 · B12 · B-complex · Vitamin C · Calcium
NAD+

NAD+ Infusion Protocol

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+) infusion for cellular energy metabolism support, mitochondrial function, and neurological optimization. Appropriate for longevity-focused patients and those with chronic fatigue.

Core Components NAD+ · Supporting B vitamins · Hydration base
Recovery

Athletic Recovery & Performance Protocol

Structured post-exertion recovery infusion combining hydration, amino acid replenishment, and anti-inflammatory micronutrients. Designed for competitive athletes and high-output individuals.

Core Components Amino acids · Glutathione · Zinc · Magnesium · B-complex · Hydration
Detox / Antioxidant

Glutathione Push Protocol

Intravenous glutathione — the body's primary endogenous antioxidant — for cellular detoxification support, skin brightness, liver support, and oxidative stress reduction.

Core Components Reduced glutathione · Vitamin C · Saline push

Clinical Note: All protocols listed above are subject to individualized modification based on your health history, current medications, lab values, and clinical objective. Not all protocols are appropriate for all patients. Final protocol selection is made by Dr. Pavlik following a medical consultation. Compounded IV preparations are sourced exclusively from licensed, FDA-registered pharmacies.
