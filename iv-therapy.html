Our Position

IV therapy is medicine.
We treat it that way.

The IV therapy market has drifted toward entertainment — drips marketed as hangover remedies, beauty boosters, and party services. At Epic Revive, that is not our practice.

Intravenous infusion bypasses the gut and delivers compounds directly into circulation. That means faster onset, higher bioavailability — and a higher standard of clinical oversight. Every formulation at Epic Revive is developed by a physician who has administered IV therapy in intensive care units, surgical suites, and pain management settings.

Your infusion here looks like it belongs in a clinic. Because it does.

Pharmacy-Sourced

All compounds sourced from licensed, regulated compounding pharmacies — never unverified suppliers.

Physician Protocol

Clinical Setting

Infusions administered in a private clinic environment with emergency equipment and a trained physician on site.

Questions

What patients ask

Standard hydration and nutrient protocols typically run 45 to 90 minutes. NAD+ infusions are administered slowly and may require 2 to 4 hours depending on dose. All infusion rates are clinically determined to optimize safety and tolerability.

When administered in a proper clinical environment by qualified practitioners using appropriate protocols, IV therapy carries a low risk profile. At Epic Revive, that standard is maintained rigorously. We screen patients for contraindications, administer at correct infusion rates, and are equipped to manage adverse events.

Frequency depends entirely on clinical indication. We do not recommend a default maintenance schedule for all patients. After your intake assessment, Dr. Pavlik will provide a clinically reasoned recommendation based on your labs, symptoms, and goals.

No referral is required. However, all new IV patients complete a medical history intake with Dr. Pavlik before their first infusion. This is non-negotiable — it is not optional and it is not an upsell. It is a clinical standard.
