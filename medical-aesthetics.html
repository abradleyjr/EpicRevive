Medical Aesthetics

Injectables and advanced skin treatments administered with the anatomy expertise of a board-certified physician — and a restrained, refined aesthetic.

Physician-Administered Why It Matters

Anatomy-First
Aesthetic Medicine

Aesthetic outcomes are directly proportional to the injector's depth of anatomical knowledge. Dr. Pavlik brings a level of anatomical precision to aesthetic medicine that is rare in this field.

As a board-certified anesthesiologist, Dr. Pavlik has spent two decades with an intimate understanding of facial anatomy, vascular structures, and tissue planes. This knowledge base translates directly into safer, more precise aesthetic outcomes.

At Epic Revive, every injectable treatment is administered by Dr. Pavlik personally — not delegated to a nurse or aesthetician. Your face is not a training exercise.

"Aesthetic medicine practiced poorly is obvious. Practiced correctly, it is invisible — the patient simply looks like a better version of themselves." Ross Pavlik, M.D.
Services Offered

Injectable & Skin
Treatment Menu

All treatments below are performed by Dr. Ross Pavlik, M.D. Consultations are required prior to all injectable procedures.

Neurotoxin

Botox & Dysport

Precision neurotoxin placement for dynamic wrinkle reduction. Treatment areas include forehead lines, glabellar complex, crow's feet, brow lift, lip lines, masseter reduction, and hyperhidrosis.

Results are calibrated for natural expression — not a frozen or artificial appearance. Dosing is individualized to your facial anatomy and desired outcome.

Physician-administered. Consultation required. Results typically 3–4 months.

Dermal Fillers

Hyaluronic Acid Fillers

Volume restoration and structural enhancement using premium HA fillers (Juvederm, Restylane product lines). Appropriate for lip augmentation, cheek enhancement, nasolabial fold softening, under-eye hollowing, and jawline definition.

Deep anatomical knowledge ensures filler is placed at the correct tissue plane — critical for both aesthetic outcomes and vascular safety.

Physician-administered. Consultation required. Duration varies by product and area.

Biostimulators

Sculptra & Radiesse

Collagen-stimulating biostimulatory agents for gradual, natural-appearing volume restoration and skin quality improvement. Appropriate for patients seeking longer-duration results and enhanced tissue quality.

Biostimulators require a protocol-based treatment series and are suited for patients committed to a multi-visit aesthetic program.

Physician-administered. Consultation required. Results develop over 2–3 months.

Advanced Skin

Medical-Grade Skin Treatments

Chemical peels and medical-grade skin protocols for skin quality optimization, texture improvement, and tone correction. Treatment selection is based on skin type, Fitzpatrick classification, and specific concerns.

All skin protocols are overseen and recommended by Dr. Pavlik following a medical skin assessment.

Physician-overseen. Protocol varies by skin type and treatment goal.
