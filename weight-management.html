The Clinical Approach

Metabolic Medicine
Without Compromise

Weight is a metabolic issue — not a discipline issue. Our protocols address the underlying physiology driving weight resistance and fat retention.

At Epic Revive, weight management begins with a comprehensive metabolic assessment. We evaluate hormonal status, insulin sensitivity, inflammatory markers, thyroid function, and body composition before recommending any intervention. Every protocol is physician-designed and physician-monitored.

We utilize GLP-1 receptor agonist therapies where clinically appropriate, combined with metabolic optimization strategies that address the full scope of your physiology. The result is not rapid, unsustainable weight loss — it is structured, measurable metabolic improvement.

"Weight management that produces lasting results requires understanding the metabolic system — not simply suppressing appetite." Ross Pavlik, M.D.
Clinical Protocols

Our Weight Management
Program Structure

Each patient receives a protocol structured around their individual metabolic profile, health history, and goals.

Comprehensive Metabolic Assessment

+

All weight management patients begin with a full metabolic workup, including body composition analysis, fasting metabolic panel, comprehensive hormone panel (thyroid, sex hormones, cortisol, insulin), and inflammatory markers. This assessment forms the clinical foundation of your personalized protocol.

A physician consultation follows to review findings and establish individualized treatment parameters. No protocol begins without a thorough clinical understanding of your baseline physiology.

GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Therapy

+

GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1) receptor agonists represent the most significant advancement in evidence-based weight management medicine in decades. These agents work at the neurological and metabolic level to regulate appetite, improve insulin sensitivity, and promote fat utilization.

At Epic Revive, GLP-1 therapy is initiated and titrated under direct physician supervision. Dosing protocols follow clinical evidence, not convenience. Regular monitoring ensures both efficacy and safety throughout the course of treatment.

Medications prescribed only where clinically appropriate following medical evaluation.

Metabolic Optimization & Lifestyle Architecture

+

Pharmacologic intervention alone does not produce lasting metabolic change. Our weight management protocols integrate evidence-based nutritional strategy, movement programming, and sleep optimization alongside medical therapy.

We do not prescribe generic meal plans. We develop individualized nutritional architecture that works in alignment with your metabolism, your lifestyle, and your medical protocol.

Ongoing Monitoring & Protocol Adjustment

+

Metabolic medicine is not static. As your body responds to treatment, protocols are adjusted to reflect your evolving physiology. Regular biomarker monitoring, body composition reassessment, and physician follow-up appointments are built into every weight management program.

Direct physician access is available to all active weight management patients throughout the duration of their program.
