Our Position

Weight is a medical issue, not a willpower issue

Obesity and metabolic dysfunction are complex, multifactorial conditions with well-established clinical pathways. At Epic Revive, we approach weight management the way we approach every clinical challenge: through assessment, data, and evidence-based intervention.

GLP-1 receptor agonists represent one of the most significant advances in metabolic medicine in decades. We deploy them with the precision and oversight that their pharmacological profile demands.

This is not a medically-supervised crash diet. This is a structured, physician-directed metabolic reset — monitored and adjusted in real time by a board-certified physician.

GLP-1 Therapy

The science behind
sustainable results

GLP-1 receptor agonists work by mimicking naturally occurring gut hormones that regulate appetite, insulin secretion, and gastric motility. The result is a clinically validated reduction in caloric intake, improved glycemic control, and measurable changes in body composition.

At Epic Revive, GLP-1 therapy is never prescribed in isolation. It is integrated into a comprehensive metabolic protocol that includes baseline lab assessment, ongoing monitoring, and dose titration based on clinical response — not a standing formula.

Clinical Framework

How our protocol works

Every patient begins with a thorough clinical baseline. There is no universal protocol — only protocols tailored to your physiology.

01

Comprehensive Baseline Assessment

Full metabolic panel, hormonal markers, body composition analysis, and a detailed clinical history. We measure before we prescribe — every time.

02

Individualized Protocol Design

Dr. Pavlik reviews your data personally and designs a therapeutic approach specific to your metabolic profile, starting dose, and treatment goals.

03

Structured Titration & Monitoring

Medication is titrated according to clinical response — not a predetermined schedule. Dose adjustments are made by the physician based on ongoing assessment.

04

Nutritional & Lifestyle Integration

Pharmacotherapy works best alongside evidence-based nutritional guidance. We provide structured dietary frameworks that complement, not compete with, your medication.

05

Ongoing Biomarker Tracking

Regular lab follow-ups track not just weight but the markers that matter: lipid profiles, glucose regulation, hepatic function, and lean mass preservation.

06

Long-Term Sustainability Planning

The goal is a new metabolic baseline — not dependency on a drug. Your exit strategy is part of the program from day one.

Candidacy

Is this program
right for you?

Our weight management program is appropriate for adults who have struggled with sustainable weight loss despite genuine lifestyle effort, and who meet clinical criteria for GLP-1 therapy or metabolic optimization.

We do not treat patients seeking rapid cosmetic weight reduction without clinical indication. Our practice is medical — and we hold the threshold accordingly.

—

BMI ≥ 30, or BMI ≥ 27 with comorbidities

Including hypertension, type 2 diabetes, hyperlipidemia, or sleep apnea.

—

History of sustained lifestyle effort

Diet and exercise modification attempted without achieving sustainable results.

—

No contraindications to GLP-1 therapy

Determined through comprehensive medical history and lab screening during your intake evaluation.

—

Commitment to the full protocol

Results depend on adherence to monitoring, follow-up, and the integrated lifestyle framework.
