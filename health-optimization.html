Program Philosophy

Preventive medicine with
executive precision

Most annual physicals are designed to catch what has already gone wrong. Our health optimization program is built around a different question: what is currently suboptimal, and what does that mean for your trajectory over the next decade?

Using advanced biomarker panels, functional assessments, and physician-directed interpretation, we build a complete picture of your current health status — and a structured plan to improve it.

This program is not a supplement stack. It is a clinical engagement — the kind of medical attention that has historically been available only to those with access to executive concierge medicine.

Who We Serve

This program is built for people who take their health seriously

Health optimization is not for everyone — not because it is exclusive, but because it requires a particular kind of patient: someone willing to engage, track, and act on data.

High-Output Professionals

Executives, founders, and senior leaders who understand that cognitive and physical performance is directly tied to their ability to operate at their best — and who want their healthcare to reflect that.

Program Structure

Four phases of engagement

Health optimization is a process, not an appointment. Our program is structured to build progressively, with each phase informed by the one before it.

Phase 01

Comprehensive Baseline

Full biomarker panel, body composition analysis, lifestyle assessment, and an extended physician consultation to map your current baseline across all six domains.

Phase 02

Data Review & Planning

Dr. Pavlik reviews all results personally and develops a structured intervention plan with specific, measurable targets — not generic lifestyle advice.

Phase 03

Active Intervention

Protocol implementation: targeted supplementation, pharmaceutical support where indicated (HRT, peptides, metabolic agents), and structured lifestyle architecture.

Phase 04

Monitoring & Iteration

Quarterly biomarker reviews and physician check-ins to assess response, adjust interventions, and track progress against your established baseline.

Hormone Optimization

TRT & Hormonal
Rebalancing

Hormonal imbalance — in testosterone, thyroid function, cortisol, or other axes — has measurable consequences for energy, cognition, body composition, and mood. These are not lifestyle issues. They are clinical findings that require clinical management.

Where labs and symptoms support a diagnosis of hormonal insufficiency, Dr. Pavlik develops individualized hormone optimization protocols using evidence-based approaches, with ongoing monitoring to ensure both efficacy and safety.

—

Testosterone Replacement Therapy (TRT)

Physician-prescribed and monitored TRT for men with confirmed hypogonadism — not an off-label shortcut to performance enhancement.

—

Thyroid Optimization

Full thyroid panel analysis and, where indicated, targeted thyroid support that goes beyond the TSH-only standard of most primary care settings.

—

Adrenal & Cortisol Support

Assessment of the HPA axis and targeted interventions for patients with documented cortisol dysregulation affecting energy and resilience.

—

Peptide Therapy

Physician-directed peptide protocols (CJC/Ipamorelin, BPC-157, and others) for recovery, body composition, and cellular repair — where clinical evidence supports use.
