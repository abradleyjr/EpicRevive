Program Structure

How the Program Works

Comprehensive Health Assessment

+

The foundation of every health optimization program. A 60-minute physician consultation combined with a comprehensive biomarker panel. Includes detailed health history review, lifestyle assessment, and establishment of your individualized baseline.

Results are reviewed in a dedicated follow-up consultation. You receive a written protocol document outlining your findings, priorities, and recommended interventions.

Ongoing Optimization Program

+

For patients committed to sustained health optimization, a structured ongoing program provides quarterly physician consultations, biannual comprehensive labs, and continuous protocol refinement as your physiology evolves and your goals develop.

Direct physician access is included for active program patients. Protocol updates are issued as new evidence emerges or your biomarkers shift.

Hormone Optimization Integration

+

Where clinically indicated, hormone optimization — including testosterone replacement therapy, thyroid optimization, and adrenal support — can be integrated into the health optimization program under direct physician management.

All hormone therapies at Epic Revive follow evidence-based dosing protocols with regular monitoring. We do not prescribe supraphysiologic doses or operate outside the boundaries of evidence-based medicine.

Hormone therapy initiated only following comprehensive baseline evaluation and physician consultation.

Targeted Supplementation & Protocol Design

+

Evidence-based supplementation is an underutilized tool in preventive medicine. Where your biomarkers indicate deficiency or suboptimal status, we prescribe targeted, pharmaceutical-grade supplementation with documented rationale.

We do not recommend supplementation without clinical justification. Every supplement in your protocol is there for a specific, measurable reason.
